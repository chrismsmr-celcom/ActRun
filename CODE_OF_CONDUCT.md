# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ActRun is a technical project that depends on frank, specific feedback. We want that feedback to be direct and respectful.

Expected behavior:

- Critique ideas, code, and designs, not people.
- Be specific and constructive. "This will deadlock in this case" helps. "This is bad" does not.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Welcome newcomers and answer their questions patiently.
- Accept disagreement gracefully. The maintainers make the final call on project direction, and will explain it.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and pull requests.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block contributors who violate this code of conduct.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@chrismsmr-celcom](https://github.com/chrismsmr-celcom), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the reporter's identity will not be shared without consent.
